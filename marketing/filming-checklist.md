# Filming Checklist

## Record These Phone Clips

Use vertical video only.

1. Dirty corner in front of house or shop, 3 seconds.
2. Same place after cleaning, 3 seconds.
3. Kit on table: grabber, gloves, bags, 4 seconds.
4. Close-up of hand wearing glove, 2 seconds.
5. Close-up of grabber picking paper or bottle, 4 seconds.
6. Person putting litter in bag, 4 seconds.
7. Full bag tied, 3 seconds.
8. Phone showing before/after upload idea, 3 seconds.
9. Older father/grandfather holding or using kit, 4 seconds.
10. Final product shot with clean place behind it, 4 seconds.

## Simple Filming Rules

- Film in daylight.
- Keep camera steady.
- Clean safe litter only: paper, bottle, plastic.
- Do not show medical waste, glass, or dangerous items.
- Put the product in the first 5 seconds.
- Keep background Tunisian and real: house, shop, street, neighborhood.

## Reel Export Settings

- Size: 1080 x 1920
- Duration: 20-30 seconds
- Captions: always on screen
- Music: low volume
- Voiceover: clear Tunisian Arabic
- End card: Ndhaf Tounes + website/order message
